SEO & AEO Playbook for Creators

Create content that answers real questions clearly and helps people discover your work.

Pillar 01 // Search Engine Optimization

SEO

Make useful content easier for search engines to understand and surface. Focus on clear topic hierarchy (H1/H2/H3), descriptive titles, internal linking, and matching search intent.

Pillar 02 // Answer Engine Optimization

AEO

Structure helpful answers so answer-driven search experiences can understand and potentially reference your content. Lead sections with a concise 40–60 word direct answer before diving into deeper context.

Pillar 03 // Durable Authority

Content strategy

Build around audience questions, search intent, original insight, and practical solutions. Pair AI research speed with your own lived client/creator examples.
